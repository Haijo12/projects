## 🚀 Getting Started

Just start typing — everything **saves automatically**. Wrap words in _simple symbols_ to style them. ==No buttons needed, no menus, no fuss.==

You write it, the app makes it beautiful.

## 🎨 Text Styles

Make your words stand out:

- **Bold** with double asterisks
- _Italic_ with underscores
- ~~Strikethrough~~ with tildes
- ==Highlight== with equals signs
- `inline code` with backticks

Combine them like a pro: **==important==** or _~~reconsidered~~_
